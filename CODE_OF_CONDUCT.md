# Code of conduct

Trayload is a small project made by people who like games and emulation. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to emulation or to GitHub.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Keep feedback specific and useful: say what happened and what you expected.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure.

## What is not welcome

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Asking for, offering or linking to pirated games, BIOS files, firmware or encryption keys. Trayload does not provide or link them, and neither does this project's space. Posts that do will be removed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or open a private security advisory from the **Security** tab and say it is a conduct report.
