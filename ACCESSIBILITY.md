# Accessibility

Trayload should be usable without a mouse and readable at different sizes. This page says what exists now and what does not.

## What is supported

- Keyboard navigation through the sidebar, library and dialogs, with a shortcuts list in the app (press `?`).
- Basic controller navigation.
- Buttons, dialogs, progress bars and the "Now playing" and copy notes have text labels or ARIA roles for screen readers.
- Music can be paused, skipped or turned off at any time, and pauses while a game runs.

## Known limits

- Trayload is Windows-only and has only been tested on Windows 10 and 11.
- It has not been tested with every screen reader. Reports are welcome.
- The interface is English only, and colors follow one dark theme.
- Some third-party emulators that Trayload launches have their own accessibility limits, which Trayload cannot change.

## Reporting a barrier

Open an issue with the **Bug report** form and say what you tried to do and what stopped you. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
